Step 2 · Logo concepts

Three directions

A · Chrome wordmark

The main logo, for the site header, chest prints and the neck label. The chrome works best on dark garments; a flat one-color version covers screen printing.

B · Angel cursor (icon)

A mouse pointer with wings and a halo. Use it for the favicon, social profile picture, hat embroidery, enamel pins and phone charms. It still reads at 16 px.

C · Burned-CD badge

A shiny CD badge with an "ab" monogram, for stickers, packaging seals, hang tags and the back neck print. The packaging seal is what customers will post in unboxing videos.

These are drafts and use web fonts. Once you pick one, I'll convert the lettering to outlines so printers and embroiderers get clean vector files.
