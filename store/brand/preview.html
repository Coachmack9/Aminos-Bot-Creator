Brand board · draft 2 · cozy loungewear, ages 17–29

STAY IN. STAY SOFT.

New name: SOFT HOURS. It names the time you spend in comfort: slow mornings, late study nights, lazy Sundays. It's easy to say, works as a hashtag (#softhours), and sounds as relaxed as the clothes.

Step 2 · Logo concepts

Three directions

A · Puffy wordmark

Rounded lowercase with a little butter-yellow moon. Use it for the site header, chest and leg prints, and the neck label. Embroiders cleanly.

B · Cloud clock (icon)

A cloud with clock hands set to "no rush." Use it for the favicon, profile picture, beanie and cap embroidery, and sock cuffs.

C · Stay In Club label

A stitched label in sage. Use it for the back neck, side-seam tags, hang tags and the packaging sticker.

These are drafts and use web fonts. Once you pick one, I'll convert the lettering to outlines for print and embroidery files.

How the brand talks
