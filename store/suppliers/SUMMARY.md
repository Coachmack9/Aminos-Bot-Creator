# SOFT HOURS: Supplier Summary (updated 2026-11-11)

Brand: cozy loungewear for ages 17–29. Mid-tier prices ($30–60, accessories under $30). Print-on-demand on Shopify, with a $100 sample budget.
Full data and a source URL for every claim are in `suppliers.csv` (30 rows). "Ask" means the fact isn't published and goes in the outreach questions.

> **Verify before ordering.** Direct page fetches were blocked in this session, so every figure comes from search-result snippets. Recheck prices and terms on the live pages.

## How suppliers were ranked
1. Fabric softness and weight
2. Cost per item against our retail price
3. Sample discounts
4. Shopify connection
5. Private label

Small U.S. shops were preferred, but most of them don't publish prices, so Printful is used as the cost baseline.

## Fleece hoodies, crewnecks, sweatpants and matching sets
1. **Apliiq (Los Angeles CA). Best for private label.**
   - **Fabric:** Bella+Canvas 4737 heavyweight sweatpants (listed as 10 oz 60/40 fleece), Independent Trading fleece joggers, IND4000 heavyweight hoodie.
   - **Decoration and labels:** embroidery on demand, woven and printed neck labels, branded mailers and packing slips.
   - **Shopify:** has a Shopify app.
   - **Open questions:** per-item prices: Ask. A 12-piece minimum appears on some pages: Ask whether on-demand orders are exempt.
2. **Printful. Best for published cost and sample discount.**
   - **Fabric:** Cotton Heritage M2580 hoodie (8.5 oz, 65/35) and M7580 fleece sweatpants (8.5 oz). The two pair up as a matching set.
   - **Price:** hoodie about $27.84, sweatpants about $29.98, plus U.S. shipping from about $8.79.
   - **Samples:** 20% off sample orders, 1 order a month, up to 3 items.
   - **Caveats:** a large platform, not a small business. Inside labels are harder to set up.
3. **Monster Digital / TSC Miami (Miami FL).**
   - **Fabric:** soft organic Stanley/Stella blanks.
   - **Shopify:** through Printify or Order Desk.
   - **Red flag:** some Printify production is in Mexico, so confirm U.S. fulfillment for each item.

Runner-up: **MerchLoop (Las Vegas NV)**: 8.5 oz embroidered hoodies, a Tultex 80/20 fleece hoodie, and private label.
