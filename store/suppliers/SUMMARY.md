# Supplier Shortlist: Summary

Prepared by Supplier Scout, 2026-11-07. The full data, with a source URL for every claim, is in `suppliers.csv` (27 rows: 18 vetted U.S. suppliers and makers, 4 excluded, 1 channel, 4 benchmarks).

> **Verify before relying on these.** Direct page fetches were blocked in this session, so every fact comes from web-search results that quote the supplier's own pages or named reviews. Anything not published is marked **Ask**. No small U.S. POD supplier publishes net-30 terms. The only verified net terms are Faire's net-60, which covers buying stock, not POD.

## 2. Hats
1. **Fulfill Engine (STAHLS')**: $0/mo entry plan, embroidery and patches, no minimums, Shopify, custom pack-ins.
2. **Apliiq**: garment-washed and USA-made trucker caps, woven hat labels.
3. **Broken Arrow Wear** (DTF hats with no minimum, 6-piece embroidery minimum) or **Personal Graphics** (Petoskey MI, no embroidery minimum). Neither has a Shopify app.

## 3. Bags (weakest category)
No small U.S. supplier was found that prints crossbody or mini shoulder bags on demand.
1. Apliiq: ask about totes and mini bags.
2. Printed Mint / MerchLoop: ask about bags.
3. Quality Logo Products: USA-made canvas totes with a "low minimum" (bulk fallback).

## 4. Accessories
1. **Sticker Mule**: $1 sample pack, 10 holographic stickers for $9, about 4-day turnaround, no Shopify connection.
2. **ShineOn**: jewelry made in the USA, Shopify app, 25% off samples (per a third-party review), white-label shipping.
3. **HarperCrown** (Los Angeles): charms, 5% off a $50 cart up to 15% at $1,000, ships same or next day.
- **Faire** (wholesale channel): net-60, $5k–$20k credit for new stores, free returns on a first order with each brand.

## 5. Packaging (phase 2; only works if the POD partner accepts mailers you supply)
- **EcoEnclose**: free poly mailer samples (you pay shipping), branded mailers from 500 units.
- **Packlane**: sample kit and small self-serve runs (high cost per unit).
- Labels: use Apliiq's in-house labels.

## 6. Cut-and-sew (phase 2, once sales prove out)
- **Lefty Production Co** (Los Angeles): flat-rate sampling in 5–10 days; MOQ listed as 50, 200 or quote.
- **Euphoric Colors / La Branders**: studio tier has no minimum up to 50 pieces.

## Excluded
PODpartner (based in China), Subliminator (Netherlands address), Only Caps (couldn't verify), IconEcom (Shopify app delisted).

## Benchmarks (large platforms, for price comparison only)
Printful (Comfort Colors 1717, Bella+Canvas 4810GD) · Printify (Premium 20–33% off, custom neck labels) · Gelato (Gelato+ $19.99/mo, up to 25% off) · Gooten (garment-dye and mineral-wash tees).
