6. **Shopify.** Do you have a Shopify app, or do you accept forwarded orders (CSV/API)?
7. **Timing.** What are your typical production and shipping times, and where do you ship from?

If it's easier, a price sheet or a short call works too. Thanks for your time. We'd love to build the brand with a small U.S. shop.
