# Brand Brief (shared by all store agents)

- **Brand:** SOFT HOURS (tagline: *Stay in. Stay soft.*)
- **Concept:** One-brand cozy loungewear line, sold online only (DTC e-commerce).
- **ICP:** Older teens and twenty-somethings (ages 17–29), U.S.-based, shop on TikTok / Instagram, mobile-first. They value comfort and "soft life" self-care: slow mornings, staying in, study sessions, travel days.
- **Aesthetic:** Cozy and calm. Oversized fits, brushed fleece, heathered and washed colors, rounded puffy lettering, clouds and moons, warm neutrals with soft color pops (sage, butter, lavender).
- **Product categories at launch:**
  - Apparel: fleece hoodies, sweatpants/joggers, crewnecks, boxy tees, matching sets
  - Hats: beanies, dad caps
  - Bags: totes
  - Accessories: crew socks, scrunchies, stickers
- **Supplier requirements:** U.S.-based small businesses offering startup incentives: sample packs, net-30 terms, first-order / new-brand discounts, low MOQs, private label / custom tags.

## Confirmed decisions
- **Price tier:** Mid ($30–60). Tee $34, crewneck $48, sweatpants $52, hoodie $58, beanie $28, tote $30, socks/scrunchies under $20. Matching sets sold as a bundle (hoodie + sweats) with a small discount. Watch POD cost per item to protect margin.
- **Inventory model:** Print-on-demand (POD), no upfront stock. Suppliers must connect to Shopify or accept forwarded orders, and support soft fleece blanks + private label (inside-neck print or custom tag, branded packing slip).
- **Platform:** Shopify.
- **Startup budget:** $100 for samples.
- **Name:** SOFT HOURS (lead pick; a quick web search found no clothing brand using it).
- **Direction change (2026-11-11):** switched from Y2K retro (ANGELBYTE) to cozy loungewear. The old work is kept in git history.
