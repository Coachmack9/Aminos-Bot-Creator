# Brand Brief (shared by all store agents)

- **Concept:** One-brand fashion line, sold online only (DTC e-commerce).
- **ICP:** Older teens and twenty-somethings (ages 17–29), U.S.-based, shop on TikTok / Instagram, mobile-first.
- **Aesthetic:** Y2K vintage — early-2000s nostalgia: baby tees, low-rise, butterfly/star motifs, chrome and iridescent finishes, bubble and blackletter type, washed and faded colors, flip-phone / digicam era vibes.
- **Product categories at launch:**
  - Apparel: tees, baby tees, hoodies, crewnecks, track pants
  - Hats: trucker hats, dad caps, beanies
  - Bags: totes, mini shoulder bags, crossbody
  - Accessories: jewelry, hair clips, phone charms, stickers, keychains
- **Supplier requirements:** U.S.-based small businesses offering startup incentives: sample packs, net-30 terms, first-order / new-brand discounts, low MOQs, private label / custom tags.

## Confirmed decisions
- **Price tier:** Premium (tees ~$60+, hoodies ~$110+, hats ~$45+). Requires heavyweight / garment-dyed blanks, quality prints, custom labels and packaging.
- **Inventory model:** Print-on-demand (POD) — no upfront stock. Suppliers must integrate with Shopify or accept order forwarding, and support premium blanks + private label (inside-neck print / custom tags, branded packing slips).
- **Platform:** Shopify.

- **Business name:** ANGELBYTE (tagline: Heaven on dial-up)
- **Primary logo:** Concept A, chrome wordmark (`store/brand/logos/A-chrome-wordmark.svg`)
